Why this exists
---------------

The Android app bundles firebase-messaging, a proprietary AAR, to receive push
notifications when a Parent Node is unreachable over the tailnet. The GPL
requires that the entire combined executable be conveyable under the GPL, and
the copyright holders of CryLog hold no such rights over that blob.

The "System Libraries" exception in GPLv3 section 1 does not cover this: it
would at most apply to Google Play Services as a preinstalled component, not to
a library deliberately added to the bundle.


Scope
-----

This additional permission applies to the Android app in android-app/.

The Hub in hub/ links no Google library — it speaks to FCM over plain HTTPS and
signs its JWT with the Node standard library — and is therefore covered by the
GNU GPL version 3 or later with no exception.

A future "foss" build flavour replacing Firebase with UnifiedPush/ntfy would
likewise require no exception.

This permission addresses licensing only. It has no bearing on F-Droid, which
rejects proprietary blobs in its builds as a matter of policy regardless of how
they are licensed.
